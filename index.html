Mechanical Wheel

A free custom picker for names, meals, games, and everyday decisions. Add your choices, give the wheel a spin, and see where it lands.

How to use the wheel
  1. Open Edit wheel to add 2–50 choices, with up to 12 characters each. Use Batch edit to paste a list.
  2. Press Spin to spin the wheel. Hold longer before releasing for more force. You can also focus the button and use Space or Enter.
  3. Choose Share to copy a link to your wheel. The latest spin can be replayed from the link.

Your choices and the last 30 completed spins are saved in this browser when local storage is available. Open Spin history to replay a result. No account is needed.

The wheel needs JavaScript and a browser with WebGPU support.
